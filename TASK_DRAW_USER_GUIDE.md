# 🎴 Task Draw — Complete User Guide & Operational Manual

Welcome to **Task Draw**, the dynamic paired task-and-discipline card engine designed for Dominant-submissive dynamics. Whether drawing daily random tasks or executing specific Dom assignments, this guide details every feature, control, and rule.

---

## 👑 1. Dominant (Dom) Controls & Oversight

As the Dominant (Sir/Mistress), you have complete control over the deck configuration, active assignments, and live oversight of your submissive's progress.

### 🌸 Active Sub Roster Switcher
- Located at the top of the screen when logged in as a Dom.
- Allows you to seamlessly switch between multiple paired submissives.
- Switching subs automatically updates the **Activity Feed**, **Live Task Status**, **Assign Card Picker**, and **Deck Rules** for the selected sub in real time.

### 📋 Pair Activity Feed
- Displays real-time logs of tasks drawn or assigned.
- Shows total **edge counts**, **completion moods**, and **submissive notes**.
- **Quick Feedback Pills**: Tap pills like `"Good girl"`, `"Again"`, or `"+2 edges next time"` to instantly send structured feedback to your submissive's notes.

### 🎴 + Assign Card Modal
- Tap **`+ Assign Card`** to override random draws and mandate a specific card.
- **Rich Card Details**: Tap **`▶ Tap for Details`** on any card to review:
  - 📖 **Instruction:** Full task instructions (personalized with your name).
  - 🛠️ **Gear Required:** Required items (*e.g., blindfold, collar, timer, rope*).
  - ⚠️ **Safety Note:** Relevant safety and physical boundaries.
  - 📋 **Rule:** Execution constraints for the task.
- Tap **`Assign`** (or **`Force Assign`**) to push the card directly to your submissive's screen.

### 🚫 Unassign Active Card
- When your submissive has an active or live card session open, a **`🚫 Unassign Active Card`** button appears in the feed header.
- Tapping **Unassign** immediately cancels the active session in real time, returning the submissive to their idle deck screen.

### ⚙️ Deck Rules & Pack Configuration
- Tap **`⚙️ Deck Rules`** to customize deck parameters for the active submissive:
  - **Pack Toggles:** Enable or disable specific packs (*Home, Dice, Writing, Bondage, Short, Long, Public*).
  - **Soft-Banned Cards:** View and clear soft-banned cards (cards the sub skipped or requested a cooldown on).

## 🌸 2. Submissive (Sub) Experience & Daily Routine

As the submissive, your goal is to draw tasks, execute instructions faithfully, log your progress, and report completion.

### 🃏 Drawing a Card
- Tap **`Draw Task Card`** from your Idle Deck screen.
- A card is drawn from active, enabled packs based on your Dom's deck rules.
- **Cooldown History:** Cards drawn recently are placed on a 8-card cooldown to ensure variety.

### 🔄 Redraw Tokens
- You receive **2 Redraw Tokens** per day.
- If you draw a card that cannot be completed due to circumstances, tap **`Redraw Card`** to burn a token and draw a replacement card.
- The skipped card is automatically soft-banned to prevent immediate re-drawing.

### 👁️ Discreet Mode
- Toggle **`🔒 Discreet Mode`** at any time.
- Masks sensitive task card titles on screen with *`🔒 Task Card (Tap to reveal)`* so you can safely view your device in public or shared spaces.

### ⏱️ Live Session & Edge Tracking
- Tap **`Begin Task Session`** when you start the task.
- **Timer:** Keeps track of elapsed task duration.
- **Edge Counter:** Tap **`+1 Edge`** each time an edge is completed during the task.
- **Finish Session:** Tap **`Finish & Complete Task`** when your task duration or instructions are fulfilled.

### 🚨 Emergency Safeword Button
- Located at the bottom of the Live Session view.
- Tapping **`🚨 SAFEWORD / EMERGENCY STOP`** immediately terminates the active card session, clears live timers, and alerts Sir/Mistress.

### ✍️ Completion Mood & Aftercare Log
- After completing a task, select your completion mood:
  - 😊 *Fulfilled*
  - 🤤 *Submissive / Melted*
  - 🥵 *Challenged / Exhausted*
  - ⏩ *Skip Next* (adds card to soft-ban list)
- Add optional reflection notes or completion details before tapping **`Send to Sir & Finish`**.

## 🛠️ Quick Troubleshooting & Best Practices

1. **Modal Sheets Not Visible**: If a modal overlay appears hidden or doesn't pop up, ensure your browser page is hard-refreshed (`Ctrl + F5` or `Cmd + Shift + R`).
2. **Switching Active Subs**: If you are a Dom with multiple submissives, use the top dropdown menu to switch active view contexts.
3. **Real-time Sync**: All card draws, assignments, timer ticks, and unassignments sync instantaneously via Firestore realtime subscriptions.
